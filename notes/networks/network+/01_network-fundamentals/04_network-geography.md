# Network Geography

When we talk about network geography, we gonna talk about smallest network, up to the
largest network.

OK, let's start from the smallest:

## Personal Area Network(PAN)

Smallest type of wired or wireless network which usually **covers a distance about 3
meters or less**.

**Examples**: The network between my laptop and my airpod that are connected using
bluetooth. Also, usb is another example of a PAN. When I plugin my usb into my laptop,
it creates a serial connection, which is actually a network(:question:). Also, the
network between the laptop and monitors, webcams, and microphones consider as a personal
area network.

> I like to think of a personal network area, as when I open my hands and spin around,
> that's about my personal area network distance. Maybe a little bit more.

## Local Area Network(LAN)

Connects components in a limited distance generally up to about 100 meters. Each segment
can only go up to 100 meters if you are using cat five cabling, but if you use fiber
optic cabling you can go further. Think about LAN in a small office or a floor of a
building.

**Examples**: This is the network at your office or your home. The internal wired or
wireless network in the office or the classroom. You are sitting in your local area
network inside my house when writing this, making a connection between all my devices
and they are in the house as part of the LAN.

My network can be consist of `Ethernet` or `Wi-Fi`. Wi-Fi is related to the
`IEEE 802.11` standard and ethernet refers to the `IEE 802.3` standard.

## Campus Area Network(CAN)

A building-centric LAN that is spread across numerous building in a certain area. It can
covers several kilometers. It can consists of different LANs connected together.

**Examples**: College campuses network, business parks, military bases.

## Metropolitan Area Network(MAN)

Connects locations that are scattered across the entire city. This can cover 40
kilometers(25 miles) or even a bit more. It can consists of CANs connected together.

**Examples**: The different departments of your city, for example the police department.

## Global-Wide Area Network(GAN)

This is the biggest area network. It connects geographically disparate internal
networks. It consists of lease lines or virtual private networks that are tunneled
through the internet(:questions:). A WAN covers a large geographic area.

> Internet is a WAN. In fact, this is the largest area network.

**Examples**: We can connect different LAN networks of governmental offices and creates
a WAN.

> WAN doesn't have to be public like the internet.
