# Network Fundamentals

In this lesson we are going to observe the network fundamentals. When we see the term
`networks`, we start to think about the internet, the fucked up internet situation in
our country, or the router of the house.

Networks doesn't limit to these things. They encompass a diverse range of connections
extending to both wireless(wifi and cellular) and wired(fiber optics and ethernet)
networks.

**Networks serve the essential purpose of connecting a multitude devices that facilitate
a seamless flow of data.**

> Bluetooth forms a personal area network(PAN) because of the proximity of the two
> devices.

*We connect to the wifi using wireless access point which is another form of network
that we call local area network(LAN)*

Also, we can have a laptop that connects to a switch, and that switch connects to a
router and that router connects to a modem which that modem connect to the internet.

> Over time our various type of networks have converged.

We have different networks for different purposes, one for calling, one for our TV, one
for video conferencing and so on. Now we rely on converged network design that can
handle diverse activities, like file sharing, video streaming, chatting, etc, because
everything is treated as a packet inside a larger IP network.

## High Availability

High availability and uptime are super important when we are designing and maintaining
our networks.

> 100% availability is unrealistic. A high goal can be five-nines(99.999%) of
> availability which allows 5 minute downtime for each year.
