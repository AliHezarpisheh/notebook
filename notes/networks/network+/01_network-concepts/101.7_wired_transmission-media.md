# Wired Transmission Media

## IEEE 80.2 (Ethernet) Standards

Once upon a time, the IEEE, the institute of Electrical and Electronic Engineers, in the
second month of 1980 (802), had a conference and they had different committee, and each
committee generate their standards. Now when you hear 802 this and 802 that, it refers
to this historical event. Many standards have later came out after that conference, but
we still refer to them as 802 standards.

The group that worked on the Ethernet, worked on the 802.3 standard.
