# Exam Tips

Here are some exam tips. Um, I know, I am not an exam guy, but these might be helpful.
After all, you are learning networking domain and topics using `network+` exam.

- There are no trick questions.
- If the words are **bold**, *italic*, or UPPERCASE, you have to pay attention to it.
- Choose the textbook answer not your own or your workspace answer.
- Choose the most correct answer. Most of the cs questions are situational, I know, but
  for the exam, choose the most correct one. Choose the answer that is correct most of
  the time.
- What is the key concept in the question? Don't fight with the question, identify the
  key concept and answer based on that.
- Don't memorize.
- When are you getting your certificate? OK, for me, I should know when should I finish
  the lessons?
