# Accessibility

Accessibility is a product, service, environment, or digital content attribute which
focuses usability by everyone, especially people with disabilities, ensuring they have
the same opportunities to perceive, understand, navigate, and interact with the world as
those without disabilities.

## Linux is For Everyone

Linux have three answers for people with physical complications:

- `AccessX` helps people with physical problems to use keyboard/mouse.
- Visual settings help with vision problems by magnifying the screen and adding
  contrasts and things like that.
- Assistive technologies like text-to-speech(tts).

These options are available in display managers and in major desktops. We have an
`Accessibility` section in Gnome configuration located at the settings. Take a look at
it yourself, I haven't brought the options here.

### TTS

Applications like `Orca` or `Emacspeak` can read the dialog boxes to you.

> We have a thing named `Braille Display`. It output text as braille for visually
> impaired people.

### Voice Recognition

This is one of the best tools people with disabilities can use. Siri is one.
