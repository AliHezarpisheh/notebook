# How to Think in Computer Science?

One of the main abilities that I should acquire learning and mastering a subject or a
field, is how to think in the certain way that the field is thought and created (by
thought).

Every subject, every content, and every book are forms of thinking. Here, I explain my
and other ways on how we should think in computer science. Every heading gives us a lead
or ask a question, which lead us to think more like a computer scientist expert.

## Everything Decision Involves Trade-offs, Choose what you want to loose

Every damn decision involves a trade-off. No matter what, we're always trading off
something for something else.

- In algorithms and data structures decision making, we can choose speed over space,
  modification speed over access speed, and so on.
- In software engineering decision making, we can choose reliability over redundancy,
  security over performance, and so on.

## Follow Conventions, Unless You Have a Clear Reason Not to

Rigidly following social conventions, can turn you into a dogmatic and traditional
person. Most social conventions had been created to bring and maintain order, but they
can get away our individuality(every decision involves a trade-off!).

Programmatic conventions serve the same purpose. They are created to bring order and
convenience for us. Although, as same as social conventions, following programmatic
conventions dogmatically is not the best idea because it can take away our freedom and
creativity, but unless you have a clear reason for not following programmatic
conventions, you have to follow them. There are no individuality involved here!
